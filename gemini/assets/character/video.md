# Gemini 猫耳女孩视频

原有片段保留原来的主题名。后来补的几段按动作改了名。全部已转成 webp。

## 待机

- `待机/平常.webp`：站着轻微晃动，耳朵、尾巴和星星挂饰轻轻动，适合当循环待机。

The chibi cat-ear girl with blue-to-purple gradient hair stands still, her ears twitching and her fluffy tail swaying slowly from side to side. She blinks gently, and the star-shaped crystal charms sparkle and swing. Static camera, clean white background, soft anime style, loopable motion.

- `待机/日常.webp`：安静站着，周围有细小闪光。
- `待机/伸懒腰.webp`

The chibi cat-ear girl raises both arms above her head and stretches like a cat, yawns cutely, then relaxes and rubs one eye. Her tail sways lazily and her wide sleeves ripple. Static camera, white background, smooth anime animation.

## 招呼

- `招呼/打招呼.webp`：面向镜头打招呼。

## 情绪

- `情绪/好奇.webp`

The chibi cat-ear girl tilts her head curiously, her large purple eyes sparkling as if she has discovered something interesting. Her ears perk up toward the camera and her tail curls upward. Static camera, white background, anime style.

- `情绪/惊鸿一瞥.webp`：看向镜头，像忽然注意到什么。
- `情绪/生气又软下来.webp`

The chibi cat-ear girl puffs out her cheeks, crosses her arms and stomps one foot with her ears pinned back and tail fluffed up. After a moment she peeks sideways, her expression softens, her ears slowly rise and her tail begins to wag again. Static camera, white background, cute anime animation.

## 动作

- `动作/喵喵喵.webp`：举起爪子，做出喵喵的样子。
- `动作/跳一跳.webp`：轻轻一跳。连跳两下，两次都是一样的闭眼笑。白底。
- `动作/追尾巴.webp`

The chibi cat-ear girl notices her fluffy tail, turns to chase it in small circles, and almost catches it. Her gradient blue-purple hair swirls around her, her ears bounce, and the star charms swing. She stops dizzy, wobbles a little, then giggles. Static camera, white background, playful anime animation.

- `动作/扑过来.webp`

The chibi cat-ear girl crouches low, wiggles her hips with her tail swishing and her eyes locked forward, then pounces toward the camera with both hands out. Her long hair and wide sleeves flutter behind her, and the star charms trail sparkles. She lands in a cute pose and smiles. Static camera, white background, dynamic anime animation.

## 互动

- `互动/被拎起来.webp`：被拎起来时耳朵先贴下去再竖起，举爪扭一下，然后放松。白底。
- `互动/拖拽.webp`：先站着，再被拎在画面中间晃，蹬腿甩尾巴，然后软下来。白底。

## 特殊

- `特殊/星星魔法.webp`：抬手，星星闪光绕在身边。
- `特殊/接住星星.webp`

Glittering star-shaped crystals fall slowly from above like snow. The chibi cat-ear girl holds out both hands, catches one, and looks at it in amazement as it glows in her palm. She then blows it away like a kiss and it bursts into sparkles. Her hair and tail float lightly. Static camera, white background, magical anime style.
